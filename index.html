ThreadFit for Reddit

Find the Reddit threads where your product fits

A Chrome extension that reads your subreddits and Reddit search, ranks every thread by how likely a reply is to land, and drafts a reply you edit and post yourself. It never posts for you.

  • High intent: people asking for what you make, with the reasons each thread scored what it did
  • Drafts written only from the facts you give it, in your own voice
  • Subreddit rules checked before you reply or post, and a timer so you don't come across as spam
  • Mentions of your product and your competitors, tagged and sorted
  • Your data stays in your browser: no account, no server, no tracking

ThreadFit is an independent tool, not made or endorsed by Reddit.
